5 Golden Rules of Indian Cyber Safety
1
Never Scan QR or Enter UPI PIN to Receive Money: QR codes and PINs are strictly used for PAYING money out. You never enter a PIN to receive payments.
2
Never Install APK Files Sent on WhatsApp: Electricity boards and govt departments never send .apk files on WhatsApp. These are remote screen-control trojans.
3
No Night Electricity Cutoffs on Personal Calls: Discoms (BESCOM, UPPCL, etc.) never send sudden 9:30 PM cutoff notices asking to call personal mobile numbers.
4
Verify Traffic e-Challans Exclusively on .gov.in: Only check fines on official echallan.parivahan.gov.in. Ignore SMS with .xyz or .top links.
5
Golden Hour Rule - Call 1930 Immediately: If you lost money, dial the National Cybercrime Helpline 1930 within 2 hours to freeze stolen bank transfers.
